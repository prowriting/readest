# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers using phones, tablets, desktops, web browsers, and e-ink devices to keep and read ebooks and audiobooks. They need a dependable library and an immersive reading or listening experience that works with keyboards, touch, screen readers, and constrained displays.

## Product Purpose

BookArc Reader brings ebooks and audiobooks into one cross-platform library, preserves reading progress and annotations, and makes opening, claiming, organizing, reading, and listening feel immediate and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and reader-first. The interface should feel familiar and quietly polished, with content taking priority over application chrome.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative or attention-seeking chrome, gratuitous motion, dense control walls, color used without meaning, inconsistent platform affordances, and loading states that leave users unsure whether the app is still working.

## Design Principles

- Keep the book and the reader's current task at the center of every surface.
- Make system state and long-running work explicit, timely, and reassuring.
- Prefer familiar, consistent controls over novel interaction patterns.
- Preserve a coherent experience across touch, keyboard, screen reader, and e-ink use.
- Use brand color and motion only when they communicate priority or state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support full keyboard navigation and common screen readers including TalkBack, VoiceOver, NVDA, and Orca. Maintain visible focus, sufficient contrast, meaningful status announcements, reduced-motion behavior, RTL-safe layout, and e-ink-readable boundaries that do not depend on color alone.
