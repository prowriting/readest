BookArc is where readers and authors connect.

Authors can send you claim codes for their books and audiobooks. Read them in the BookArc app or web reader, or send them to Kindle, Nook, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Kobo, and other reading apps.

You can also discover thousands of classic ebooks and read them for free.

Authors can share early copies of their books with you. Support their work with thoughtful feedback and honest reviews that help their books succeed.

BookArc creates a stronger, more direct connection between readers and authors, reducing the middlemen and fees that often stand between them.

- Read in the BookArc app or on the web
- Send books to your preferred reading app or device
- Claim books sent directly by authors
- Explore thousands of free classic ebooks
- Receive early copies and share feedback with authors

There is no cost to readers. No in-app purchases. No ads. Just your favorite books.
