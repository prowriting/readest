Version 0.12.8 (12023) improves claim saving and adds Send to Kindle to the library. For an eligible EPUB, PDF, or TXT, long-press the book in Library and choose Send to Kindle. The iOS share sheet opens; select Kindle if installed. Canceling leaves the book saved. Author-restricted app-only books and audiobooks do not offer Kindle export.

BookArc is a free reading app. Readers receive books and audiobooks directly from authors using claim codes, and can also read public-domain classics from the built-in Discover catalog. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.

No sign-in is required to review the app. Claiming, reading, listening and Discover work without an account. Cloud sync and account management require optional sign-in. If you want to try Sign in with Apple, any Apple account works; it creates a BookArc account on the spot (no demo credentials are needed).

How to test:
1. Claim tab: enter one of the claim codes below. These review codes are reusable and do not expire. Choose Save to My Library to save and return to Library, or Start Reading to save and open the reader.
2. Library tab: open the claimed book to read it.
3. Discover tab: browse and open any free classic.
4. Audiobook: claim the audiobook code below, then open "Moby-Dick" from the Library tab to test playback: background audio, the lock screen, and CarPlay (audio app). In the CarPlay simulator the app lists the audiobook and plays it.
5. Optional: Sign in with Apple from Library > "Sign in to sync your library". Account deletion is under Library > menu > Account > Delete Account; it removes the account and all cloud data and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.

Purpose, audience and business model: BookArc lets independent authors deliver ebooks and audiobooks directly to readers through claim codes, including gifts, advance review copies and newsletter bonuses. It is for general adult readers of fiction and non-fiction. The reader app is free; authors use the separate BookArc author platform to upload and distribute their own content.

Core external services: our BookArc Reader API and author platform on Microsoft Azure; Azure Blob Storage and Azure SQL for files and account data; optional Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In; SendGrid for account emails; opt-in PostHog analytics and crash reports. Optional reader integrations include Readwise, Hardcover, KOReader sync and WebDAV.

Regional differences: the app functions consistently across regions; claimed content depends on the codes supplied to the reader. Discover offers public-domain classics.

Content rights: authors or publishers upload claim-code books and confirm they hold the rights when uploading. Each delivered copy is watermarked for its recipient. Discover contains public-domain works, including Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks titles.

This build also fixes claim links opened while another book is being read, so the claim dialog remains open.
