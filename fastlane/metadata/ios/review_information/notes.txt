BookArc is a free reading app. Readers receive books and audiobooks directly from authors using claim codes, and can also read public-domain classics from the built-in Discover catalog. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.

No sign-in is required to review the app. Every feature below works without an account. If you want to try Sign in with Apple, any Apple account works; it creates a BookArc account on the spot (no demo credentials are needed).

How to test:
1. Claim tab: enter one of the claim codes below. Each code is single-use; several are provided. The book is added to your library on this device.
2. Library tab: open the claimed book to read it.
3. Discover tab: browse and open any free classic.
4. Audiobook: claim the audiobook code below, then open "Moby-Dick" from the Library tab to test playback: background audio, the lock screen, and CarPlay (audio app). In the CarPlay simulator the app lists the audiobook and plays it.
5. Optional: Sign in with Apple from Library > "Sign in to sync your library". Account deletion is under Library > menu > Account > Delete Account; it removes the account and all cloud data and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.

Claim codes (single use):
- "Pride and Prejudice" (ebook): 3QF7ZHZ, SJE7EPT, 36D7KY6
- "Moby-Dick" (audiobook): 9R8EBWR
