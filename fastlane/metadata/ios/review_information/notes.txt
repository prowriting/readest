BookArc is a free reading app. Readers receive books and audiobooks directly from authors using claim codes, and can also read public-domain classics from the built-in Discover catalog. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.

How to test:
1. Sign in with the demo account above, or tap "Sign in with Apple" (the app supports Sign in with Apple natively).
2. Library tab: the demo account already has "Pride and Prejudice" claimed.
3. Claim tab: enter the claim code below to receive an additional book from an author. Each code is single-use; several are provided.
4. Discover tab: browse and open any free classic without an account.
5. Audiobook playback supports background audio, the lock screen and CarPlay (audio app). Audiobook delivery is in a limited rollout; the demo account's audiobook ("Moby-Dick") is available if it appears in the Library tab, and CarPlay then lists it.

Demo claim codes (single use, each delivers "Pride and Prejudice"): 3QF7ZHZ, SJE7EPT, 36D7KY6

Account deletion: Library > menu > Account > Delete Account. This removes the account and all cloud data and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.
